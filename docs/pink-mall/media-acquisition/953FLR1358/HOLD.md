## Why it is held

1. `953FLR1358` returns no product anywhere. It matches no retailer listing, no
   catalogue entry and no manufacturer reference.
2. *Paws & Whiskers* is not a distinguishing brand here. Several unrelated pet
   businesses trade under that name, and none of them lists this article.
3. The photographs contradict the supplied brand. A woven tag is sewn to the
   cushion in frames 01 and 03: a short single wordmark, blue lettering on
   orange, with a small animal mascot to its left. It is legible as a mark but
   not resolvable as text at the supplied resolution, and it is plainly **not**
   "Paws & Whiskers" — the shape is one short word, not a two-word ampersand
   name.

Publishing would mean printing a brand attribution that the owner's own
photographs appear to contradict. Consistent with the Kartell `KL080V`
decision, the product is held rather than published on an unverified name.

## What is established

The photographs themselves support: a hook-over radiator or shelf cat bed, two
wooden side panels with paw-print cutouts, a round pink sherpa cushion, and a
width of **52 cm** printed as a dimension overlay on the third frame. None of
this identifies a manufacturer.

## What would unblock it

Any one of:

- the brand as printed on the packaging, the care label or the supplier invoice;
- a legible photograph of the sewn tag;
- a supplier or retailer link showing this exact article.

With the brand confirmed by the owner, this publishes as `OWNER_CONFIRMED` —
the route already used for the Lanvin bucket hat (PM-042).
